The story / 01

The work started in New York in 1991.
Then life went elsewhere.
Now we’re making things again.

A small garage turned sewing shop in Brooklyn, near Coney Island. That was the beginning: patterns, fabric, cutting and sewing.

OLLO is a return to something we used to love doing: making clothes. The old scissors are still here. So are the habits—draw, cut, fit, change, make it again.

We are starting small.
